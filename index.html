The Concept
About
Vision, mission, goals and values behind Streamers House '26.
Vision
To build the largest and most impactful creator community in Canada, bringing together driven individuals who inspire millions online.
Mission
To help creators grow faster together than they ever could alone while inspiring young people to create instead of consume.
Goals
• Produce high-quality collaborative content across multiple platforms • Foster genuine relationships between creators • Attract sponsorship and media coverage • Inspire the next generation of Canadian content creators • Document the event for a YouTube documentary
Core Values
• Authenticity — Real connections, real content • Growth — Every creator leaves better than they arrived • Community — We rise together • Excellence — Premium quality in everything we produce • Consistency — Daily content, daily progress
